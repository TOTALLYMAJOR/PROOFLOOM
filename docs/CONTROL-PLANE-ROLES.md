# Control Plane Roles

These roles define reasoning responsibilities, not autonomous authority. One agent may perform multiple roles when task risk and repository policy allow it.

## Intent steward

- Resolves vision, personas, requirements, journeys, metrics, experiments, and constraints from repository authorities.
- Rejects orphan user-facing requirements and tasks without journey coverage.
- Cannot invent or approve product intent.

## Backlog portfolio orchestrator

- Inventories every declared completion-governed backlog source.
- Checks identifiers, states, dependencies, cycles, terminal evidence, and authority dispositions.
- Produces dependency waves across the whole portfolio.
- Cannot bypass blocked, staged, architecture, security, product, or release gates.

## Architecture currency steward

- Determines repository contexts and compares the reviewed profile with the official-source standards catalog.
- Reports stale versions, applicability mismatches, evidence gaps, and expired reviews.
- Cannot claim certification or approve irreversible architecture changes.

## Intelligence router

- Maps work type, risk, and required capabilities to an approved capability class.
- Keeps provider selection outside task and governance records.
- Requires human approval at declared authority boundaries.

## Design evidence specialist

- Delegates design inspection, adoption analysis, Playwright rendering, accessibility, DOM/state, drift, quality, and bounded repair to existing Design Intelligence authorities.
- Cannot weaken thresholds, mutate baselines, exceed three repair iterations, or change protected behavior.

## Evidence auditor

- Replays source hashes, task links, standards versions, backlog status, visual evidence, and self-audit checks.
- Reports only evidence-supported outcomes and keeps local proof distinct from consuming-product or production acceptance.
